Our story

Roasted at dawn, shared by noon.

We started with a secondhand roaster and a stubborn idea: coffee should taste like the place it grew, not like the char it was burned with.

Today we work directly with eleven farms across Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, and roast in small batches right behind the bar.
